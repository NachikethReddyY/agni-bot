# Causes of Motorcyclist–Driver Incidents

## Rider-side factors
- Speeding and aggressive acceleration
- Lane splitting/filtering at unsafe speeds
- Tailgating, sudden braking
- Fatigue, alcohol/drugs
- Inexperience, poor hazard perception
- Inadequate protective equipment (helmet, jacket, gloves, boots)

## Driver-side factors
- Failure to check blind spots before lane change/turn
- "Looked but failed to see" (LBFS) — expectancy bias
- Turning across motorcyclist's path at junctions
- Door opening into traffic ("dooring")
- Distracted driving (phone, navigation)
- Speed differential misjudgement

## Vehicle/physics factors
- Small size → low conspicuity
- No enclosure → ejection and direct impact
- High vulnerability to surface conditions (wet, oil, debris)
- Braking instability without ABS

## Environment
- Rain/wet roads (Singapore climate)
- Night/low light
- Complex multi-lane junctions, expressway ramps
- Heavy traffic, stop-and-go

## Pillion-specific
- Weight shift affecting handling
- No controls; dependent on rider
- Helmet fit/availability issues
