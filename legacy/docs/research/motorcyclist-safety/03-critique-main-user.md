# Critique: Main User = Enclosed Vehicle Users?

**Position: Reject as sole main user; adopt dual-user framing.**

## Why not enclosed vehicle users as main user
- Inverts accountability: the protected party becomes the one with least
  physical risk.
- Risks victim-blaming riders for systemic visibility/road-design issues.
- Driver-only solutions (e.g., "drivers must watch out") leave rider
  agency, skill, gear, and infrastructure unaddressed.
- Many incidents are single-vehicle or rider-error; driver-centric design
  misses those.

## Why enclosed vehicle users still matter
- Multi-vehicle collisions dominate fatalities.
- Drivers control the larger, more lethal mass; their attention/behaviour
  is a major causal lever.
- ADAS and driver training can materially reduce "looked but failed to see".

## Recommended framing
- **Primary beneficiary:** motorcyclist (and pillion) — protection,
  conspicuity, skill, gear, infrastructure.
- **Co-responsible actor:** enclosed vehicle user — detection, yielding,
  safe manoeuvres, technology adoption.
- Design interventions that require action from both; measure outcomes for
  riders, not driver convenience.
