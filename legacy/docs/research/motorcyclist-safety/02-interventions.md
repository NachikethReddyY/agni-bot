# Candidate Interventions

## Rider-focused
- Enhanced training (hazard perception, defensive riding, wet-weather)
- Conspicuity: high-vis gear, daytime running lights, reflective tape
- Mandatory ABS on motorcycles
- Helmet standards enforcement; pillion helmet provision
- Fatigue/alcohol enforcement

## Driver-focused
- Blind-spot mirror checks, shoulder checks before lane change
- ADAS: blind-spot monitoring, AEB for two-wheelers, turn-across-path detection
- "SMIDSY" awareness campaigns targeting drivers
- Stricter penalties for failure to yield to motorcycles

## Shared/infrastructure
- Motorcycle-only lanes or advance stop lines
- Improved junction design, sight lines
- Surface maintenance, drainage
- Data-driven black-spot treatment

## Innovative
- V2X / connected vehicle alerts (motorcycle-to-car)
- Smart helmets with collision warning
- Dashcam/AI near-miss reporting for hotspots
